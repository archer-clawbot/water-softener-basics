Water Softener Buying Checklist: Questions to Ask Before You Buy

Pricing, installation scope and warranty terms for water softeners vary a lot between dealers. The easiest way to protect yourself is to ask the same questions of everyone and get the answers in writing.

About your water

  • Was my water tested, by whom, and can I have the full results?
  • Does the test show anything besides hardness that this system won't address?

About the system

  • What capacity are you recommending, and how did you calculate it?
  • Is it timer-based or demand-initiated?
  • What brand and model is the control valve, and who can service it?

It helps to run your own numbers first. The water softener sizing calculator lets you check whether a quoted size is in a sensible range.

About the price and installation

  • Is the quote itemized for equipment, installation and any permits?
  • Does installation include a bypass valve, drain connection and any needed plumbing changes?
  • Are there ongoing fees, and are they optional?

About the warranty and service

  • What does the warranty cover on the tank, resin and valve, and for how long?
  • Does it cover labor, or parts only?
  • What happens if the dealer goes out of business?
